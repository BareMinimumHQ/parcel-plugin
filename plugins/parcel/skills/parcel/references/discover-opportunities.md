# Discover opportunities

Find or triage opportunities through the provider's available search, browsing or authorized email capabilities. Parcel stores selected Jobs. Explain unavailable source access and work from supplied listings; monitoring and scheduled searches require separate supported provider capabilities and authorization.

Read current Job Preferences and relevant Career Profile. Requirements are objective assessment inputs, Priorities are weighted outcomes, and optional Search guides discovery and narrative review. Ask only for essential missing direction; supplied postings can go directly through [capture job posting](capture-job-posting.md). Derive useful title variants and keywords without persisting them as preferences.

Use titles as search anchors, along with industries, company sizes, desired or avoided work and screening guidance. Give preferred companies attention and omit excluded companies from discovery. Company preferences do not override Requirements unless the person's guidance authorizes an exception. An explicitly supplied listing can still be captured, with its relation to saved direction explained when relevant.

Apply posting age at search time, not email receipt time, and check the employer's posting date and current state where available. An undated listing cannot be confirmed within an age window. Prefer active employer sources and verify role identity, location eligibility and compensation meaning.

Distinguish confirmed Requirement failures from missing or ambiguous evidence. Missing salary remains unknown unless the person specifies a stricter screening policy. Apply authorized stretches and conditional rules without fabricating failed factual assessments or dismissing transferable experience for a tool-name mismatch. Search informs recommendations, not Match scores or readiness; use [assess Match](assess-match.md) for formal assessment.

Check saved Jobs for duplicates and present a bounded shortlist with source links, fit evidence, material gaps and tradeoffs. State the search sources and bounds actually covered; partial or failed access does not prove no opportunities exist. The person chooses what to pursue. Capture selected Jobs when authorized; discovery alone does not authorize contacting employers, submission or status changes.
