# Prepare a Cover Letter

Use the `parcel` MCP tools to read the Job, Saved Job Posting, current Cover Letter, relevant Career Profile and Job Resume. Use Company research when useful and distinguish sourced facts from inference. Ask for missing material that prevents a credible letter. Do not invent personal experience, achievements, company facts, contact names, addresses, or reasons for interest.

Read the saved Account Cover Letter writing preferences before drafting. Apply explicit instructions for this request ahead of saved guidance. A request-specific override does not authorize changing the saved preferences. Resume writing guidance and its saved page target do not apply to the Cover Letter.

Read the role before choosing company context. Prefer a few specific links between the role's needs and truthful experience, outcomes and motivation. Use current sourced company information only when it helps explain that connection; do not pad the letter with company boilerplate or praise. Write the letter in the person's first-person voice and keep wording natural. Ask for genuine motivation when it is missing, rather than inventing it.

Draft a concise letter with specific, supported connections between the person's experience and the role. Preserve useful existing wording when revising; avoid generic praise and unsupported claims. Fit the content through focus and concise wording before proposing changes to template, typography, or other appearance settings. A length request does not authorize appearance changes.

Follow the draft review, document fit, and save rules in the Parcel skill before treating this material as ready.

Show the proposed content for review and save only with authorization. Supply the letter content through the current discovered operation. Parcel handles creation and internal structure. Preserve unrelated content when revising a saved letter and follow the common conflict and safe retry rules. Render the confirmed saved letter and verify readability, layout, and any agreed length. If the person wants its PDF, display it; an unsaved draft is not the saved PDF.

Do not submit externally, publish documents, change Job status, or claim that saving sent an application.
