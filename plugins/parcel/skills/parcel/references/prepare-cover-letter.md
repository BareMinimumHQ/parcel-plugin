# Prepare a Cover Letter

Read the Job, Saved Job Posting, current Cover Letter and relevant Career Profile and Job Resume. Use Company research where it helps explain the connection to the role.

Write a concise, natural first-person letter connecting the person's supported experience and outcomes to the role's needs. Use sourced company context where relevant; avoid generic praise and company boilerplate. Ask for genuine motivation when it is missing rather than inventing reasons for interest.

When revising, preserve useful existing wording and focus on the requested change. Present the proposed content and material open questions.

Use the shared draft-rendering loop to check length before an authorized save. Saved content and unsaved proposals use the same document renderer; a prepared PDF address alone is not evidence of rendering or fit.
