# Capture a Job posting

Identify the intended Job and read existing details and Posting before changing them. Check for duplicates before creating a Job; clarify the target when uncertain. Ask for a URL or pasted description when no source is available.

Retrieve the source with the host's browsing capabilities; Parcel does not fetch posting URLs. Prefer the employer's original listing and retain its source URL. Extract supported facts into Job details separately from the saved Posting. Check whether the listing is active and explain closed, expired or unavailable sources without automatically changing saved Job status.

Save all readable content belonging to the Posting word for word and in its original order, regardless of its headings or section names. Convert HTML or Markdown presentation to plain text, removing only formatting and unrelated surrounding page content. Normalize spacing and line breaks without changing source wording or punctuation; retain link text and destinations in plain text. Include only material from the source. Keep your own contributions outside the saved Posting.

If retrieval is incomplete, fails, or exceeds the capture limit, disclose that and ask for complete pasted text or another complete source. Do not silently truncate, substitute a summary, or replace existing saved text with an incomplete extraction.

When extracting structured Job details, keep compensation bounds, currency and period, remote-location eligibility and other restrictions clear. An upper salary bound is not a lower bound; ambiguous currency or omitted details stay unresolved rather than being guessed or silently converted.

Present the proposed Job details and Posting for authorization under the shared save rules. Keep them consistent with the approved proposal and report partial saves accurately. Capturing evidence does not establish that a Job passes Filters, refresh an existing Match, or imply external application or a status change.
