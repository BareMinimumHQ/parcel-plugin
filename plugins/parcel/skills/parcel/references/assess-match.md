# Assess a Match

Read the Job, Saved Job Posting, Job Preferences and current Match with its captured inputs. Explain missing posting or meaningful preferences before scoring. A saved Match describes the posting and criteria it captured. Explain changed-source warnings and distinguish those earlier criteria from current preferences when discussing saved scores.

When asked to rerun or refresh Match, reassess the complete comparison against the intended current inputs. A retained Priority ID does not establish that its meaning is unchanged. Review every configured Requirement and Priority and save a complete assessment; do not carry scores forward because their IDs still exist. Follow the current supported reassessment workflow if an older action reports that it is retired.

Assess every configured Requirement against its own explicit posting evidence. Choose Matches (✓) only when the evidence establishes the requested fit, and Does not match (×) only when it establishes a conflict. Choose Not stated (–) when the posting gives no information about that requirement, and Unclear (?) when it says something relevant but does not resolve the fit. For example, no published pay is Not stated. A published ceiling above the target with no usable floor is Unclear. A Remote label can establish workplace arrangement without establishing geographic eligibility. Explain material uncertainty briefly. Do not mark a requirement as matching just because the posting does not rule it out.

Score supported Priorities from 1–5, where higher means closer fit. Use each saved description as its rubric and explain the evidence, score and gaps. Low (1), moderate (3) and strong (5) examples can clarify a particular preference; avoid imposing a universal rubric or changing identities and weights. Score the opportunity offered, rather than candidate qualifications, unless that Priority asks otherwise. Leave unsupported Priorities unscored. Estimated Match is Parcel's calculation, not a hiring probability; Filters do not contribute.

Use relevant Search direction to explain preferred work, stretches, company exceptions and screening policies separately from saved assessments. It cannot synthesize Filter outcomes, change scores or determine readiness, and company preference alone does not override Requirements.

Assess Experience separately using relevant Career Profile or Resume evidence. Write overlap as evidence-grounded bullets connecting supported experience to the role. Label gap bullets:

- **Soft gap:** Transferable experience with a remaining difference in tool, domain, scale, depth or responsibility. Explain both the supporting evidence and what remains to learn or demonstrate; this does not mean missing interpersonal soft skills.
- **Hard gap:** A confirmed missing required capability with no supported equivalent. Identify the requirement and evidence establishing the gap.
- **Unconfirmed:** Insufficient evidence; state what needs confirmation. An omitted profile skill does not establish a Hard gap.

Experience does not affect Estimated Match. Empty fields mean no saved assessment, not no overlap or gaps. Experience can be assessed independently of preference scores. Review its evidence during reassessment; Career Profile edits do not supply a stale indicator. Existing text can remain when that review supports it.

Present the assessment and evidence. Preserve unrelated assessments, Experience and captured sources during focused changes. An ordinary edit does not clear an outdated-source warning; adopting different assessment inputs or clearing saved work must be deliberate.
