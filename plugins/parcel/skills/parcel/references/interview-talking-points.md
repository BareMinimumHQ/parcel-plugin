# Interview Talking Points

Use the `parcel` MCP tools to find the Job and read its Posting, Match, current Resume and Cover Letter, Career Profile, authorized Notes, Company research, and existing Talking Points. Read the collection revision and limits before changing anything.

Map the role's important requirements to specific truthful evidence from the Career Profile or authoritative source Resume; ask when sources conflict. Use saved Job status and Notes for application context, not as evidence of company facts. Prioritize three to five differentiators, with concrete experience, outcome, and relevance. Surface substantive gaps tactfully and give an honest framing strategy. Distinguish transferable capability from the absence of required experience; never invent metrics, ownership, technologies, or expertise.

Prepare roughly six to eight Talking Points for this exact role, company, and known interview stage, adjusting to the requested scope and collection limits. Give each title (`topic`) one of two forms: a likely question from the interviewer, phrased as a question; or a specific topic the person should or wants to bring up. Include both forms when useful to the requested preparation. Cover relevant depth, systems or product judgment, collaboration, and behavioral evidence. Label assumptions about interviewer intent. Preparation is requested work, not an automatic consequence of finding a Job.

For either title form, write `suggestedApproach` as the person's fully developed response in first-person Markdown bullet points (`I`, `my`, `me`), as if they were saying or reading the answer aloud. Use complete spoken sentences, usually three to five bullets where the scope and field limits allow. Develop the relevant context, what I did or would do, my reasoning, supported outcomes, and why it matters for this role. Each bullet must carry a complete part of the response, not a short memory cue or a coaching instruction such as “mention your experience.” Keep the answer clear and natural without reducing it to fragments.

Propose the ordered points with why each may arise, supported experience, likely questions, difficult follow-ups, and Low, Medium, or High Priority. Keep objective company facts and preparation notes separate from the first-person response. Use honest first-person framing for adjacent or missing experience; do not invent personal motivations or turn a proposed future approach into past experience. Ask for missing facts when needed, and keep material open questions outside the spoken response.

Follow the draft review and save rules in the Parcel skill before treating this material as ready.

Saving Talking Points replaces the current collection. Show the proposed complete collection and save through `update_talking_points` only after explicit authorization, with `mode: "replace"`, `jobId`, the complete retained `points`, the expected `ifRevision` (0 if absent), and a fresh UUID `operationId`. Reuse the same operation ID only for an exact retry after an uncertain result. On `stale_revision`, read again and ask for a choice when the versions cannot be merged safely. Report the saved result only after the tool confirms it.
