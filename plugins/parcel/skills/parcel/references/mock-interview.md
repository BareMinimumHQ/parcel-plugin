# Mock Interview and Practice result

Read relevant Job and Posting context, source Resume or Profile, Company research, Talking Points and prior feedback. Clarify the interview stage or focus when it changes the practice. Ask role-specific questions one at a time and adapt follow-ups to the person's answers. Offer voice when supported by the host. Give feedback on what was actually said, with concrete strengths, gaps and next practice steps; do not invent answers or feedback.

Keep the conversation in the provider session. Parcel records reviewed feedback, never a transcript, audio, session identifier or conversation URL.

For an authorized Practice result, identify the Job and relevant existing feedback. Record the focus, date and time, covered topics, summary, strengths, problem areas, suggestions and ordered prioritized Takeaways. Each distinct practice conversation has its own result; a correction targets the existing result and preserves unrelated feedback. Recording practice does not create a calendar event, reminder or Job status change.
